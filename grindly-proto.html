02 — How it works

How it works

From signup to your first applications in minutes.

Step · 01 Drop your resume Create an account and upload your resume. That's the only homework you do.
Step · 02 Answer a few profile questions Domains, locations, stipend, daily limits. These become the agent's firewall — what it may and may not apply to.
Step · 03 Activate & choose updates Complete checkout, then pick how you want reports — email (no setup) or Slack DM. Connect Gmail to auto-detect interview calls.
Step · 04 The agent applies for you It reads listings, scores each against your resume, and auto-applies to the strong matches — within your limits.
Step · 05 Daily progress reports Every day you get a report: who it applied to, match scores, and what it skipped (and why). Update outcomes to track your interview rate.
